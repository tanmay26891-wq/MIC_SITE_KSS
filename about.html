Our story

Sunday Loaf started in a home kitchen, with one oven and a big pot of sourdough starter. Friends kept asking for loaves, then their neighbours did too. Soon, we needed a bigger oven.

Today we're still a small team. We bake in small batches, buy flour and butter from local farms and mills, and know many of our customers by name.

Good bread takes time. We're happy to wait.
